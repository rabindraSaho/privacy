LudoMate – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026

Who we are

LudoMate (the “App”) is a Ludo board game for Android, published on Google Play under the package name com.rabindra.ludogame. It is developed by Rabindra Kumar Sahoo, an individual developer based in India (“we”, “us”).

This policy explains what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

The short version

  • The game works fully offline. There is no account, no login and no game server.
  • Everything you enter in the game stays on your phone. We never receive it.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. When you are online, Google may collect device data, including your Advertising ID, to show and measure ads.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

The App saves the following only in its private storage on your phone:

  • Player names you type when setting up a game.
  • Your unfinished game, so you can continue later.
  • Game statistics such as games played and wins.
  • Your settings, such as sound and vibration.

We have no access to this data. It is never sent to us or to any server we control.

If you have turned on Android backup, Android may include this data in your own backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Google under your account settings, and we cannot access it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads: a full-screen ad when the App opens, a full-screen ad after a finished game, and a banner at the bottom of the game screen. Ads load only when your phone is online.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When an ad is requested, Google may automatically collect:

  • Your Android Advertising ID.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate location (such as country or city) can be derived.
  • Device information, such as model, operating system, language and screen size.
  • Ad interactions, such as ads shown and tapped.
  • Diagnostics about the ad SDK’s performance and crashes.

Google uses this data to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise them. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you.

Your ad choices

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where the law requires it, the App asks for your consent before showing personalised ads.
  • Change your choice: where this applies, open Settings in the App and tap “Ad privacy choices”.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it at any time in your phone’s settings, under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads on some devices).
  • Go offline: the game is fully playable without internet, and no ads load while offline.

Children

LudoMate is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App and shared personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Data saved on your phone stays until you clear it. To delete it, uninstall the App or go to Android Settings → Apps → LudoMate → Storage → Clear data.
  • Data collected by Google for ads is kept and deleted under Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it, then delete it.

Security

Your game data is stored in the App’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. Ad requests are sent by Google’s SDK over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the data we handle to a minimum.

Changes to this policy

If the App starts handling data differently, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top.
